## Finding fixed during baselining (D-06 follow-up)
# fsstress initially left 101 orphan warnings per run (all seeds, -p 1
# and -p 4 alike): allocate_sectors flushed journal prefix chunks as they
# filled, then returned No_Space and abandoned the committed prefix.
# Reproduced in-process (create + 2MB write + unlink on a 1M image).
# Fix: _allocate_rollback_best_effort frees the partial chain from its
# recorded head on every late failure (No_Space or I/O past flushed
# chunks); the new chain is always standalone (tail link runs only on
# success). Regression: tests.test_mount_oversize_write_no_leak
# (ENOSPC honest, zero drift, checker clean).
